== PAGE 9 ==
INTRODUCTION
You are an ecologist working on the planet Photospheria, and your job is 
to build a biological sample from the planet’s plant life and bring it back 
to Earth for study.

== PAGE 10 ==
THE PROBLEM
The plant life on Photospheria works through interaction. Different 
plants affect each other depending on where they are placed. 
Certain combinations allow new species to flourish.
Other combinations make it more difficult for plants to flourish. 
Because of this, what you choose to place together directly shapes 
which kinds of plants exist in your final sample.
== PAGE 11 ==
THE MISSION
Your main goal is to create the most useful possible sample for Earth’s 
scientists.
There is also a secondary goal: longevity.
Your task is simple:
• Build the best possible sample of Photospherian plant life.
• Make it as diverse as possible.
• See if you can keep as many species alive for as long as possible.

== PAGE 12 ==
PROBLEM MECHANICS
Grid Layout: 
The world is represented by a coordinate system
Ticks (Days): 
The simulation progresses in discrete time steps called ticks. Tick 1 represents Day 1. 
Tick T represents the final day. Participants may schedule planting actions on any 
tick from 0 (initial setup) to T-1, final actions occur on the penultimate day, and 
scoring is done on the state of the world on the final day.
A planting action specifies:
• The tick at which to place.
• The plant index (matching the index value in the supported plant catalogue).
• The X coordinate.
• The Y coordinate.

== PAGE 15 ==
THE LEVELS
Level 1: Greenhouse Study
You will grow your plants in a greenhouse to guard 
against animals. You will only work with a small subset 
of plants and your aim is to grow a diverse garden.
Level 2: Garden Growth Study
Now that you have the hang of things, we'll move outside into a 
small garden. Your plants are now exposed to the elements, and a 
few critters might come and say hi. To add to this, you also have a 
wider variety of plants available because you have more space.

== PAGE 16 ==
Level 3: Park Potential
Let's kick it up a notch and take it out to a park 
where we introduce plant unfriendly terrain and 
more weather events will start to take place.
Level 4: Forest
You are exposed to all the elements in 
Photospheria, and will be allowed to 
make use of an entire forest. Use it 
wisely!

SUBMISSIONS
Participants must submit their solution on the Entelect Hackathon website. The submission must 
include two items: 
• A ZIP of the source code. 
• A .json file containing the output. 
• The solution must be deterministic, meaning that given the same input, the program must 
always produce the same output. During validation, the submitted source code will be 
executed to reproduce the results provided in the submission. If the source code does not 
produce the same output as the submitted file, the submission will be considered invalid.
